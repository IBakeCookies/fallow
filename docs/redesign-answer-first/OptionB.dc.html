Four of five stop before flow. Cognitive, not clock — and no task move or budget change improves that.

what this buys

The screen matches what you are actually doing. Stop-by and flow-arrival — the two readings the model computes that nothing else in the app acts on — become the whole page instead of two table columns.

what it costs

The plan the solver computed becomes invisible. Re-planning — the thing you do when the day slips — needs a trip to another tab, and the twenty-four readings have nowhere to live on the page you look at most.
