Findings (all known-or-minor; none invalidates)

1. The midpoint is not centered by any theorem — rationality puts λ₀ anywhere in [max(0,lo), hi]. The estimator's justification is measured containment (1/307 post-censor) plus accepted one-signed bias (+0.023 pooled; +0.30 at λ₀=0.1), not unbiasedness. MATH.md says exactly this; just don't let anyone paraphrase it as "unbiased".
2. valueStd is within-model only. All midpoints share the conditioning params (V_T, α, r, ϕ-plane), so a mis-set slider shifts every day the same way; i.i.d.-days math reports ±0.05 at n=12 regardless. §8.10 flags V_T as "a real unfitted error source"; worth remembering the ± cannot see it by construction.
3. Heteroscedasticity is unweighted. Instrument error is strongly λ₀-dependent (0.30 → 0.05 across the grid) while the ridge weighs all days equally. Mostly mooted by the clock censor removing the low end, but it's the one place a weighted variant would be textbook.
4. σ̂² shrinkage artifact: SSR is measured against the shrunk MAP, so n identical points still yield positive scatter (conservative — std too wide at small n; vanishes asymptotically; shared with §8.7/§8.9 machinery).
5. Negative-marginal lo floors at 0, converting a truly vacuous bound into a fake two-sided day at (0+hi)/2 — a rare, small downward pull; the comment knows ("vacuous") but it does inflate usedCount.
6. Stipulated constants stay stipulated: σ₀=0.25 (≈ quantization 0.125 + inseparable mood noise) and margin 0.25 (flat region, censoring-nothing measured better on contaminated arms) are honestly labeled non-derived; the refusals to act are measured and recorded. Coherent.
7. Nits, no action: isClockCensored recomputes recoveredRest/join three times per day per fit (perf only, ~0.185 ms/day); a retro-edited availableHours silently moves old brackets; value0 clamping is dead code since both call sites pass the default 0.5 (verified: energy-lab-store.svelte.ts:682, session-history.ts:448 — both anchor the prior at the default, matching the doc).
